What do the types mean?
  • Blister pack — rigid clear plastic case with an illustrated card as the background.
  • Carded strap — a strap that comes with its original card or label.
  • Lobster claw — a strap with a metal lobster-claw clasp.

A piece can have more than one type, e.g. blister pack + lobster claw.
